# Analysis Methodology and Key Findings

## 1. Analytical Approach

The AuraRetail project followed a structured business intelligence workflow:

1. Data inspection and understanding.
2. Data cleaning and preparation using Power Query.
3. Relational data modelling in Power BI.
4. Development of DAX measures.
5. Exploratory data analysis.
6. Focused business investigations.
7. Executive dashboard development.

## 2. Focused Investigations

### Electronics Profitability

The investigation compared six electronic products using revenue, unit costs, discounts and gross margins.

**Main finding:** The 4K Ultra Monitor 27in generated €344,631.25 in revenue but had the lowest gross margin at 45.59%.

Its average unit cost was €210, considerably higher than the other products examined. This makes its cost structure an important area for further investigation.

### Product Returns

The investigation compared the number of orders and return rates across the six electronic products.

**Main finding:** The Ergonomic Mechanical Keyboard had the highest return rate at 11.18%, while the 4K Ultra Monitor 27in had the lowest at 9.40%.

The dataset did not contain return-reason information, limiting the ability to explain the causes of returns.

### Promotional Performance

The investigation compared promotional campaigns using order volumes, revenue, discount amounts and discount rates.

**Main findings:**

- Non-promotional orders accounted for approximately 81.7% of all orders.
- Holiday Madness '24 generated €59,746.67 in revenue, the highest among the discounted campaigns examined.
- Black Friday Cyber Week '25 recorded the highest discount rate at 30%.

The analysis highlights why promotional performance should be evaluated using profitability and incremental sales, not revenue alone.

## 3. Management Implications

The analysis identified three areas deserving further management attention:

- Review the cost structure of lower-margin electronic products.
- Monitor product return rates and improve the collection of return-reason information.
- Evaluate promotional campaigns using profitability and incremental performance.

These are areas for further investigation, not proven causal explanations.

## 4. Analytical Limitations

The project uses synthetic data and has several limitations:

- No return-reason information.
- No campaign expenditure or customer exposure data.
- No controlled baseline for measuring incremental promotional performance.
- Limited information for explaining changes in logistics and fulfilment costs.

## 5. Conclusion

The project demonstrates how multiple business datasets can be transformed into a structured analytical model and an interactive Power BI reporting solution.

It combines data preparation, data modelling, DAX development, exploratory analysis and business-focused dashboard design to support evidence-based management decisions.
